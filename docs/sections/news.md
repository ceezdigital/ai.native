# News (Daily Brief)

`src/features/news/` — renders `<section id="news">`

## Purpose

A "Daily Brief" widget styled like a live feed, proving ongoing AI/tech commentary rather than a one-off event brand. Headline is "Running commentary, not just an event brand" (the item moved here from the Why section).

## Behavior

Live pulse indicator (CSS `@keyframes pulse`, decorative only). Rows show category / headline / relative timestamp; timestamp column hides under 640px.

## Open items

Entries are placeholders following the approved category+headline+timestamp pattern — real Daily Brief headlines are not finalized yet.
